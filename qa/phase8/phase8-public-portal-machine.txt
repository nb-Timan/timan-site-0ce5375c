Configurator save and quote rules

- Gem som lead creates exactly et CRM lead and intet T-nummer.

- The first T-number is created only by the explicit Afsend tilbud action.
- Discarding an unsent configuration creates no lead, no quote and no order.
- Editing a recipient email is a lead/configuration-local snapshot change and does not change dealer master data: no.
